# Customer 360 Data Product

## Product Name
Customer 360

## Business Purpose
A trusted customer-level analytical product combining customer identity, purchase behaviour, support activity and digital engagement.

## Primary Key
`customer_id`

## Consumers
- Marketing
- Sales
- Customer Support
- Analytics

## Refresh
Daily

## Source Systems
- Customer registration
- Orders
- Payments
- Customer support
- Web activity

## Quality
The published product must pass:
- customer ID not null
- customer ID unique
- non-negative spend
- non-negative order count
- valid segment
- valid activity status

## Key Business Questions
- How much has a customer spent?
- How frequently does the customer purchase?
- When did the customer last purchase?
- Is the customer active or at risk?
- How much support activity exists?
- How engaged is the customer digitally?
